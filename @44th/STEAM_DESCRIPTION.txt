[h1]44th Battalion Framework[/h1]

The complete framework for the 44th's milsim operations; the full order of battle, the shared kit library, kit-selection crates, and supply crates, all bundled into a single mod.

Place the entire ORBAT in one click, and every slot spawns kitted, playable, and can be re-roled on the fly.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]What's Inside[/h2]

[b]44th ORBAT[/b]
The complete 44th order of battle as a one-click Eden composition; every slot playable with lobby descriptions grouped by section. Units spawn with their full role loadout automatically (no crate visit needed) and can be re-kitted on respawn. Individual units and sections can also be placed from the asset and group browsers. Includes Lead/Co Zeus curator modules.

[b]44th Kit Core[/b]
The single source of truth for every role's loadout. Both the ORBAT (spawn kitting) and the Kit Crates (customisation) read from it, so a player's spawn kit and the crate contents can never drift apart. Also enforces kit version control between server and clients.

[b]44th Kit Crates[/b]
For customising within a role's allowed parameters and for re-roling. On a player's first spawn, the kit menu auto-opens scoped to their faction. Includes a Master Kit Crate (shows each player only their own role's kit) plus per-faction crates (RBN, RBN Support, RANGER, SFSG, SRR, SAS) for Zeus to re-role on the fly.

[b]44th Supply Crates[/b]
Pre-configured supply crates for 44th loadouts; medical, fire support, precision, anti-armour, breaching/EOD, sniper ammo, and full section resupply. Placed in Eden under 44th Mods → Supply Crates.
