[h1]44th BAF[/h1]

The milsim half of the 44th Battalion Framework, built on 3CB BAF classes. Adds the full order of battle and pre-configured supply crates for every section and specialist role. Requires [b]44th Core[/b] alongside it.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]What's Inside[/h2]

[b]44th ORBAT[/b]
The complete 44th order of battle as a one-click Eden composition — every slot playable with lobby descriptions grouped by section, loadouts baked in, and Zeus modules included. Individual units and sections are also available in the asset and group browsers. Each slot spawns already wearing its full role loadout (rank and TFAR frequencies included), no crate visit needed.

[b]Units included:[/b]
[list]
[*]Regimental HQ — Lead Zeus, Co Zeus
[*]RBN (Regular Battalion) — Platoon HQ, Zulu/Victor/Juliet sections
[*]RBN Support — Sappers, GPMG, Mortar, Javelin, Sniper team, Mailman
[*]Ranger (The Cadre) — 11-man command cadre, Mailman
[*]SFSG (Special Forces Strike Group) — HQ, Zulu/Victor/Romeo/Juliet sections, Mailman
[*]SRR (Special Reconnaissance Regiment) — Recon Bricks 1–3, JSFAW
[*]SAS (Special Air Service) — Troop HQ, Bricks Zulu/Victor/Romeo/Juliet, JSFAW
[/list]

[b]44th Supply Crates[/b]
Pre-configured supply crates placed in Eden under 44th Mods → Supply Crates, or requested in-game from a 44th Logistics Point — no Zeus required.

[b]Crates included:[/b]
[list]
[*]Medical — Medical Supplies
[*]RANGER — Fire Support, Precision, Anti-Armour, 11-Man Section Supplies
[*]RBN — Sniper and Spotter Ammo, 8-Man Section Supplies, EOD/Breaching Supplies
[*]SFSG — Fire Support, Precision, 8-Man Section Supplies
[*]SRR — SRR Supplies
[*]SAS — SAS Supplies, SAS Juliet Supplies
[/list]
